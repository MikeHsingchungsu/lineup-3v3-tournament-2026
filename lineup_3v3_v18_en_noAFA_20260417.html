Game Rules
Game
Rules
Adapted from FIBA 3x3 Official Rules
3v3 Youth Basketball · 2026 Summer
FIBA 3×3
Basic Settings
Players
3 per team · All on court · No substitutes
Ball
Size 5 (all groups)
First Possession
Coin toss · winner chooses
Time-out
1 per team · 30 seconds · dead ball only
Timing & Scoring
GameDate & Time
10 minutes (stopped clock · paused on all dead balls)
Scoring
1pt inside arc · 2pts beyond arc · 1pt free throw
Shot Clock
Junior (Age 7–9) 30s · Senior (Age 10–12) 15s (if no shot clock, referee counts last 5 seconds verbally)
Victory
First to 21 points, or the team leading at the final whistle
Overtime
1-minute break · no time limit · first to score 2 points wins
Ball in Play
Check Ball
After each score, defense passes from arc center · offense begins on possession
Clearing
After score or turnover, offense must clear arc before shooting (no clear on offensive rebound)
Jump Ball
No control → last defending team gets the check ball
Foul System (FIBA Team Accumulation)
Team FoulsNon-shooting FoulShooting Foul
Fouls 1–6Check Ball1 FT (inside) / 2 FT (outside)
Fouls 7–92 Free Throws2 Free Throws
Foul 10+2 FT + PossessionSame
Unsportsmanlike Foul
2 FT (fouls 7–9) or 2 FT + possession (10+) · 2 UF = disqualified
Technical Foul
1 FT + offensive possession (shot clock reset 12s)
